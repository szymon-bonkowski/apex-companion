## Scope

1. Carbon technical graphite, Liquid layered fluid, Editorial typography-led light/dark journal and Tactical competitive telemetry. Shared tokens plus explicit variants; no four copies of repositories.
2. Instant application-wide styling, independent durable schema1 appearance, migration/corruption handling and local preset create/rename/duplicate/delete/restore.
3. Dedicated Settings → Appearance Studio with real DEMO component previews, apply/discard/previous/defaults, color/mode/OLED/dynamic accent, surface/density/geometry/type/motion/accessibility controls.
4. Today with real semantic modules, independent visibility/order/size, unavailable hiding and playlist choice. Different style defaults; no fictional matches/maps/stats.
5. Glance and WidgetKit native style/accent/density/surface overrides per instance, app-following projection and preserved state/source/time. No extra provider fetches.
6. Responsive screens, large fonts, touch/focus semantics, reduced effects and OS accessibility/power adapters. No expensive full-screen blur or native glass claim.
7. Favorite-map preferences with disabled delivery capability while authorized future data is unavailable; deterministic UTC safe planning, no DEMO/stale/speculative notifications or extra polling.
8. Regression of Maps/Ranked/history/sessions/matches/backup/local PC sync; v0.4 automatic capture remains unfinished and production autoacceptance disabled.
