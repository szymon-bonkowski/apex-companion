# Ideas beyond the committed roadmap

These are hypotheses, not approved version requirements. Promote only with verified data source, privacy review and a new version spec. Keep platform parity in scope discussions (iOS WidgetKit and Android Glance may implement differently).

## Near-term potential

- Multiple mode-specific map widgets and family styles with per-instance presets.
- Favorite map alerts with best-effort platform notifications.
- Live player lookup by authorized UID/platform; daily and weekly RP trends.
- Scenario simulation, goal probability estimates explicitly labeled as heuristics and source-backed threshold data.
- Original session summary/share cards, no unlicensed art.

## Further research

- Manually confirmed match journal and on-device screenshot OCR with per-platform pickers.
- Squad comparison from explicitly added public identities within rate budgets.
- Season Wrapped as original shareable cards.
- ALGS, Twitch Drops, updates/event calendar with licensed/verified public sources.
- Loadout/team-composition planning from versioned data, avoiding unsupported meta claims.
- Favorite skin wishlist and store alerts **only** with approved source/rights.
- Optional Wear OS extension (Android-only) and native platform quick actions.
- iOS lock-screen widget families / Live Activities and Android persistent session notification, subject to platform support and battery tests.

## Explicitly excluded or paused

- **Apex Packs and Heirloom Tracker:** entirely removed from active scope at maintainer request. Do not implement a pack counter, pack journal, shard reset, pack widget or backup schema until a new approach is explicitly approved.
- Game process/memory access, protected packet interception, anti-cheat interference or gameplay automation.
- Unbounded UID monitoring, secret scraping, redistributed copyrighted assets without rights, or bypassing API quotas.
- Inferring exact matches from RP snapshots, mandatory accounts, ads, payments or hidden user telemetry.
